## BD Motions (Full Flow)

### Motion 1: Candidate-Led / MPC Outreach
Use when we have strong candidates or redacted CVs matching a company's open role.
1. Identify strong candidate profile (ask TA team)
2. Find companies hiring for matching roles
3. Find decision-makers
4. Send personalized email / LinkedIn message
5. Offer to share redacted profiles
6. Book discovery call if interested (within a month — ask Santy)

### Motion 2: Role-Led Outreach
Use when a company has posted active job vacancies.
1. Find job vacancy
2. Understand the role and tech/non-tech requirement
3. Find relevant decision-maker
4. Pitch RemoteStar's ability to support hiring
5. Mention CTO-led and AI-powered screening
6. Book discovery call

### Motion 3: Company Hiring Support Outreach
Use for companies that may not have visible vacancies but are likely to hire.
1. Research company growth
2. Identify likely hiring pain
3. Reach out with a consultative message
4. Ask whether they have upcoming hiring needs
5. Book intro or discovery call

### Motion 4: GCC Outreach
Use for companies building or expanding GCC capability.
1. Find GCC signal
2. Identify leadership, HR, TA, or engineering decision-makers
3. Pitch RemoteStar's ability to support scalable hiring
4. Book discovery call
5. Escalate strong accounts to Santy / Naresh

### Motion 5: AI Product Demo Outreach
Use for recruiters, staffing firms, internal TA teams, or companies with high candidate screening volume.
1. Identify manual screening pain
2. Pitch RemoteStar AI product and Kai
3. Offer a demo call
4. Show how Kai conducts video interviews and provides scoring/transcripts
5. Move qualified opportunities to Santy / Naresh if needed

### Motion 6: Partnership / Referral Outreach
Use when the goal is to build referral or partner relationships.
1. Identify partner type
2. Reach out with a collaboration angle
3. Explain what kind of referrals or partnership is useful
4. Book intro call
5. Track partnership data on a spreadsheet

### Rebuttals

**We do not use agencies**
I understand. Many companies prefer to handle hiring internally. RemoteStar is different from traditional agencies because we combine CTO-led hiring expertise with AI-powered screening. We are not trying to replace your internal team; we can support as a backup for niche, urgent, or hard-to-fill roles. Would it be worth reviewing one relevant profile to understand the quality?

**We already have vendors**
That makes sense. We often work alongside existing vendors when companies need additional quality or support for niche roles. Our value is in CTO-led screening and AI-assisted shortlisting, so the focus is on sending fewer but stronger profiles. Would you be open to keeping us as a backup option for difficult roles?

**We have an internal hiring team**
That's great. We are not here to replace the internal team. We can support when the team is overloaded, when roles are niche, or when faster screening is needed. RemoteStar can act as an extension of your hiring team. Would it be useful to explore this for any current or upcoming roles?

**Not hiring right now**
No problem at all. Would it be okay if I stay in touch for future hiring needs? We can also share occasional relevant candidate profiles or AI screening updates if useful.

**Send details over email**
Of course. I'll send a short note with RemoteStar's process, client examples, and how we can support. To make it relevant, is there any specific hiring area I should focus on, such as tech, non-tech, contract, GCC, or AI screening?

**We had a bad experience with agencies**
I understand, and that is common when vendors send volume without proper screening. RemoteStar's approach is different. We are CTO-led and AI-powered, which means candidates are screened more carefully before being shared. Would you be open to reviewing one profile or seeing our screening workflow before deciding?

**Pricing / commercials?**
Commercials vary depending on the role, hiring model, and client requirement. The best next step would be a short call with our team so we can understand your needs and share the right commercial structure.

**We only need recruitment software, not services**
That makes sense. In that case, the RemoteStar AI product may be more relevant. Kai can conduct AI-led video interviews and provide scoring and transcripts, helping reduce manual first-round screening. Would you be open to a quick demo?
