## Talent Acquisition (TA)

### Process For New Roles
1. Get from client: Role, Location, Hybrid/Remote, Budget, Current Team Size, Notes
2. Add role in FreshTeam (ATS)
3. Add Technical Screening Questions in FreshTeam
4. Post job / start sourcing
5. Shortlist candidates
6. Contact via InMail, Email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Phone
7. Share profile on Slack for peer review
8. Before sharing to client: check with Account Manager

### Tools
- Headhunting: LinkedIn Recruiter Boolean Search, SignalHire, Resdex
- Job Portals: LinkedIn, Indeed, Naukri, Jobsite
- Social Hiring: StackOverflow, GitHub, FB, LinkedIn Groups, Telegram

### Candidate Outreach Sequence
1. Send InMail / LinkedIn connection request + call immediately
2. Wait 4 hours → WhatsApp / Text message
3. Wait 4 hours → Email
4. Interview slots: Outside India — 10 mins + 10 min buffer; India — 15 mins + 10 min buffer
